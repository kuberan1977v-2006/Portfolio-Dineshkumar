## 🏆 Case Studies

### Redesigning the Student Portal UX
**Client:** University Tech Services  
**Role:** Lead UI/UX Designer & Frontend Developer  
**Duration:** 4 Weeks

Students found the existing portal confusing, with poor navigation and outdated design. Support tickets related to finding information increased by 40%.

**Research:** Conducted user interviews with 25 students, analyzed heatmaps of the existing portal, and benchmarked against 8 competitor platforms.

**Persona:** Arjun, 20, Computer Science student - needs quick access to grades, assignments, and campus resources.

**Outcome:** New portal launched with a 60% reduction in support tickets and a 4.8/5 user satisfaction rating.

**Lessons:** Simple navigation beats complex features. Students value speed and clarity over advanced functionality.
